# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing E-Commerce sales data to uncover business insights related to sales performance, profitability, product categories, and customer segments.

The analysis was performed using Python and data visualization techniques to help understand sales trends and business performance.

---

## Objectives

1. Calculate monthly sales and identify the highest and lowest sales months.
2. Analyze sales by product category.
3. Perform sub-category sales analysis.
4. Analyze monthly profit and identify the most profitable month.
5. Analyze profit by category and sub-category.
6. Compare sales and profit across customer segments.
7. Analyze the sales-to-profit ratio.
